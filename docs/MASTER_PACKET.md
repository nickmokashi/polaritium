## 1. The game

An original, highly interactive, visually extraordinary 3D universe simulation. The player has
godlike influence over a mysterious substance called Polaritium: a fictional foundational medium
through which opposing forces interact, transform and produce new forms of existence. The player
does not primarily build civilizations, cities, territories or economies. The player manipulates
the underlying relationships that allow complex forms to emerge.

An immense, dark, beautiful reality of floating formations, streams of silver energy, radiant
structures, living geometry, strange organisms and intricate networks of forces. The player can
pull opposing forces together, separate them, twist relationships, change energy, induce
resonance, interrupt connections and seed new structures. The universe responds: forces cancel,
amplify, or produce an unexpected third property. Structures stabilize, oscillate, grow,
reproduce, transform, fracture, merge or collapse. Some formations develop autonomous behavior.
The central question: **What happens if I try this?**

## 2. Philosophical foundation

Inspired by The Grey Strand, never a sermon. Principles: black is not inherently evil; white is
not inherently good; grey is not automatically the ideal middle; opposing forces can be mutually
necessary; balance is contextual and dynamic; instability is not always failure; destruction can
enable transformation; creation can produce unintended limitations; complex systems can develop
properties absent from their parts; relationships can matter as much as the things related.
Explore them through working systems; never deliver conclusions; let the player discover them.
Use scientifically inspired concepts (feedback, resonance, emergence, oscillation,
non-equilibrium dynamics, adaptation, complex networks). Clearly distinguish fictional mechanics
from established science; do not assert the simulation proves mystical or scientific claims.

## 3. Strict differentiation from Eonweld

Eonweld changes geography and observes populations, civilizations and history. Prohibited as
central systems: raising/lowering terrain as the primary power; geographic maps; kingdoms or
villages; food production; wars between nations; cultural histories through geographic
determinism; Eonweld's chronological map-and-history experience; its historical ledger as the
defining mechanic; copying Eonweld code, assets, designs. Polaritium's playground is a 3D field of
forces, entities and relationships; its primary interaction is direct manipulation of them; its
reward is new behavior and forms; its exploration is moving through the realities produced.
Beings need not be human. Generic engineering (deterministic simulation, saves, tests) is allowed.
Create `DISTINCTION_FROM_EONWELD.md` and use it as a continuing design test. Do not modify Eonweld.

## 4. The primary player experience

A vast, nearly monochromatic realm: darkness, depth, subtle motion. Two concentrations of opposing
energy; a delicate silver connection occasionally appears between them. Free movement. Approaching
reveals structure; touching causes a physical response; pulling them closer strengthens their
interaction; a strand forms; as tension changes particles move, geometric structures emerge, new
patterns appear — driven by simulation state, not animation. A stable ring may form, oscillate,
gather bodies, and a small autonomous organism may emerge, attracted to the difference between the
sources; the player may nurture, observe, challenge or destroy it. Loop: Discover → Interact →
Transform → Observe → Understand → Experiment Again. The interaction itself must be pleasurable.

## 5. The three foundational expressions

Black, white and grey are expressions of the medium, not moral categories.
**Black — absorption and potential:** absorption, concealment, inward movement, compression,
retention, concentration; supports protection, storage, stability, new beginnings; excess may
produce isolation, saturation or collapse.
**White — emission and expression:** radiation, expansion, outward motion, revelation, dispersal,
activation; supports illumination, formation, communication, growth; excess may produce
dissipation, overload or instability.
**Grey — interaction and transformation:** coupling, exchange, tension, resonance, mediation,
feedback, emergent transformation; sometimes stability, sometimes violent instability, sometimes
new organization. The mathematics must allow asymmetrical, changing, non-equilibrium states; no
artificial preference for equal quantities.

## 6. The force system

Complementary relationships: Emission/Absorption, Expansion/Compression, Coherence/Variability,
Attraction/Repulsion, Growth/Dissolution, Motion/Rest, Connection/Isolation,
Stability/Transformation — built into entities, fields, links, materials and transformation rules,
not eight sliders. Example: an emitter introduces energy, an absorber collects it, a connection
establishes a transfer path whose flow depends on stored energy, resistance, distance, coupling and
surroundings; raising coupling first improves transfer, then past a threshold the system oscillates;
stable oscillation can create organized structure, or fracture and release energy — all from
defined rules. Hybrid model: Nodes, Fields, Connections (strength, directionality, resistance,
elasticity, phase, stability), Structures, Agents, Transformations, Signals. Authoritative state
separate from graphics; fixed-step updates; conservative numerics (stability limits, bounded
quantities, damping, explicit conservation or dissipation rules), documented and consistent.

## 7. Godlike interaction tools

Mainly within the 3D space, not panels. **Hand** (select, grab, move, manipulate), **Seed**
(introduce a concentration with defined properties), **Strand** (draw connections), **Pull**
(attraction, repulsion, relational distance), **Twist** (torsion, phase relationship), **Pulse**
(controlled disturbance), **Tune** (frequency, resonance, coupling), **Fracture** (interrupt or
destabilize), **Fuse** (compatible structures into a shared state or merger), **Veil** (isolate a
region from selected influences), **Lens** (reveal invisible forces and relationships), **Anchor**
(lock position, phase or property), **Time Hand** (pause, step, accelerate, return to earlier saved
states). Each tool: visual indicator, responsive feedback, direct manipulation, preview, clear
effect on state, elegant particles/motion/sound, undo where practical, keyboard shortcut,
accessible alternative to precision dragging. Never a decorative-only tool.

## 8. The Grey Strand mechanic

The visual and mechanical representation of relationships — not the game's title. Each connection
stores source/destination, coupling, energy flow, resistance, tension, phase, stability, direction
of influence, local interference. Fine luminous silver filaments: tension straightens them,
resonance makes traveling waves, instability branches/vibrates/flickers, fracture visibly releases
energy. Visualization must match stored state. A Strand Sight mode reduces background and reveals
the active network; players can follow and inspect a connection.

## 9. Emergent structures and living forms

Compositional emergence: patterns of forces produce stable structures, structures combine, some
exhibit life-like behavior. Families: **Crystalline** (grow along gradients and constraints,
branch, fracture, absorb), **Membrane** (boundaries, enclose energy, exchange, divide),
**Oscillating** (internal cycles, synchronize, interfere, resonate, signal), **Motile** (move by
environment, seek gradients, avoid disturbance, follow connections), **Adaptive** (adjust to
repeated pressure; reproduce with variation; bounded selection). Fictional life-like systems, not
claims of real life. Functional behavior from simulated properties; procedural morphology.

## 10. Autonomous intelligence without AI costs

Local algorithms only: state machines, utility systems, steering, reaction-diffusion-like rules,
cellular automata, oscillator networks, simple learning, genetic variation and selection, local
graphs. Organisms explore, absorb, respond, protect, reproduce, relate, go dormant, shaped by
internal state and environment, inspectable ("what is it responding to"). No API calls for any
ordinary behavior.

## 11. Complexity and discovery

**Discovery Atlas** records meaningful findings automatically (first stable resonance,
self-sustaining oscillation, persistent crystal, mobile organism, repeated reproduction, stable
network of interacting forms, complex chain reaction, survival of a major disturbance…), each with
name, snapshot, conditions, measurable properties, reproduction instructions, notes, optional AI
interpretation. Never lock basic tools behind achievements. A local classification engine judges
novelty, persistence, complexity and behavior with defined thresholds and references previous
findings; not every random change is a discovery.

## 38. Final creative directive

Existence emerges through relationships. Black and white are not enemies. Grey is not an
obligation. There is no correct universal ratio. Sometimes opposition creates tension, tension
creates movement, movement creates organization, organization develops the capacity to persist,
adapt or transform — and sometimes a beautiful structure must collapse before something else
becomes possible. Make those relationships playable, visible, responsive, surprising. A universe
the player can touch, change, experiment with and discover. Sophisticated underneath, intuitive on
the surface; genuine mystery, consistent rules; powerful without predetermination. The objective is
not to win; it is to encounter something extraordinary and wonder what might happen next.
The Grey Strand provides the philosophy. Polaritium provides the playground.
