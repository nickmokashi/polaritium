# Polaritium: the guide

Polaritium is a dark, quiet universe made of one strange medium. It shows itself three ways:
**light** (it gives energy out), **dark** (it draws energy in and holds it) and **grey** (it sits
between things and passes energy, rhythm and change from one to the other). None of them is
good or bad. You touch them, and the universe answers.

The question the whole game asks: *what happens if I try this?*

## Starting

1. Run `Polaritium Setup 1.0.0.exe`. Windows may say it does not recognise the publisher
   (the installer is not code-signed). Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Open Polaritium from the Start menu or the desktop.
3. Press **New experiment**, pick a place and a starting system, and press **Begin**.

A short first-steps guide appears the first time. You can skip it and bring it back later from
Settings, Accessibility.

## Moving around

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Look around | Right-drag, or left-drag on empty space with the Hand |
| Pan | Middle-drag, or Shift + right-drag |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel (it zooms toward the pointer) |
| Fly | W A S D, Q and E (Shift goes faster) |
| Focus on what you selected / follow it | F / G |
| Go back to the previous view | Backspace |
| Slow cinematic camera | C |

## The tools

The rail on the left holds every power. Number keys pick them.

| Key | Tool | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hand | Select, grab and throw. |
| 2 | Seed | Place new light, dark, grey, a crystal seed or a living form. |
| 3 | Strand | Connect two things. Energy flows along a strand. |
| 4 | Pull | Draw things toward a point (Shift pushes), or shorten and lengthen a strand. |
| 5 | Twist | Twist a strand. A twisted strand carrying flow turns its ends around each other. |
| 6 | Pulse | Send a wave through the medium. Hold longer for a stronger one. |
| 7 | Tune | Change something's rhythm (you hear it), or a strand's coupling. |
| 8 | Fracture | Cut a strand, or shake something until it may break. |
| 9 | Fuse | Merge two things into one. |
| 0 | Veil | Place a shield that keeps its inside apart from the outside. |
| L | Lens | Shows the invisible: pooled light, passing vibration, the pull on each thing. |
| K | Anchor | Hold something in place, or hold its rhythm or energy. |

Every tool works without dragging too: select something, then use **+** and **−** (Alt for
resistance), the arrow keys to move it, and **Delete** to cut or break it.

**Strand Sight** (Tab) quiets everything except the web of relationships, so you can see what
is connected to what and how strongly.

## Time

**Space** pauses, **full stop** steps one tick, **[** and **]** change speed (a quarter to eight
times). **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** undo and redo, exactly: the universe returns to the state it
was in. The clock button opens rewind, which keeps a snapshot every ten seconds. The flag button
makes a checkpoint; any checkpoint can become a **branch**, a new universe that goes its own way.

## What can happen

Nothing in Polaritium is scripted. Everything below comes out of the rules when the conditions
allow it.

- **Flow.** A strand between light and dark carries energy. More coupling carries more, until
  the flow starts to ring back and forth. Too much and the strand snaps.
- **Rhythm.** Everything has a beat. Connected things with close beats fall into step.
- **The third thing.** A strand that rings in step can give birth to grey.
- **Collapse.** A dark that can hold no more collapses and throws its energy out.
- **Rings and membranes.** A closed loop of things in step becomes a ring with its own identity.
  A strong ring can grow a membrane, and a membrane holding enough energy can quicken.
- **Crystals.** In stillness and light, crystals grow along their own lattice. Shaking them at
  their own frequency breaks them.
- **Life.** Small forms feed on light or dark, move, flash, bond, divide with variation, go
  dormant and die. Over generations, a population changes in answer to what you do to it.

Each new kind of event goes into the **Discovery Atlas** with a picture, measurements and what
caused it. The Atlas only records what really happened.

## The places

- **The Void Cradle.** A sparse dark where even weak attractions show.
- **The Resonance Cathedral.** Mirrored walls; every vibration comes back to you.
- **The Fracture Garden.** Everything breaks easily, and what breaks grows back.
- **The Stillness Well.** A heavy calm where small disturbances last a long time.
- **The Threshold.** A bright half, a dark half, and a moving edge where things change.
- **Genesis (The First Dark).** Almost nothing. The deeper laws wake only as you find them.

## Paradox Chambers

Twelve puzzles, each a question with more than one answer: Still Motion, The Delicate Thing,
Heartbeat, Through the Storm, Same and Changed, Unlikely Bond, The Choir, Dark Garden, The
Breaking, Balance Without Equality, The Long Echo, Threshold Crossing. A chamber checks its goal
from the simulation itself, so any way that truly works counts.

## Keeping things

- The game saves on its own every three minutes (change it in Settings, Data and saves), when
  you go back to the main menu, and when you close the window. **Ctrl+S** saves now.
- **Continue** on the main menu opens the last universe. **Your universes**, at the bottom right
  of the main menu, lists every one, with **Rename** and **Export** beside each.
- The **Living Gallery** keeps moments you liked; any of them can be resumed.
- **Compare** runs two experiments side by side.
- **Export** writes a `.polaritium` file you can keep or give to someone (also in the in-game
  menu, Esc). **Import a universe file** opens one.
- **Take a picture** (P) saves a PNG with or without a title, explanation, measurements or caption.

Saves live in `%APPDATA%\Polaritium`. Uninstalling the game leaves them alone.

## Optional AI

With AI off (the default), every reading in the game is local and free. If you add your own
Claude key, the game can interpret an experiment, suggest things to try, name discoveries and
write captions, and only when you press a button for it. It never spends more than the limits
you set; the default is $10 a month. See `API_SETUP.md` and `BUDGET_AND_PRIVACY.md`.

## Settings

- **Graphics:** Low, Balanced, High or Ultra, plus glow, dust, resolution and a 30-frame limit.
  Balanced is the default. On this game's test laptop (Intel UHD graphics) every level holds
  its frame limit.
- **Sound:** master, ambience, effects and interface volume.
- **Controls:** camera speeds, invert vertical look, and a no-drag mode for every tool.
- **Display:** full screen and interface size.
- **Accessibility:** reduce motion, reduce flashing, high-contrast interface, larger text and
  sound captions.

## If something goes wrong

- **The picture looks wrong or slow:** Settings, Graphics, choose Low.
- **A save will not open:** the game keeps the two previous versions of every save and falls
  back to them on its own. If the whole save file is damaged, it is set aside and the last
  daily copy is restored; the screen says so.
- **AI says it is not available:** see `API_SETUP.md`. The rest of the game is unaffected.

Polaritium uses scientific ideas (feedback, resonance, oscillation, emergence, adaptation) to
make a fictional medium. It is not a model of real physics or biology, and it does not prove
anything about the real world.
