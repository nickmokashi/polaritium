# Polaritium 1.0.0

17 October 2026. First release, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

## What is in it

- **A living medium.** Light, dark and grey concentrations, strands that carry flow, rhythms
  that fall into step, collapse, overload and the birth of grey from a ringing strand.
- **Emergence.** Rings with lasting identities, membranes, quickening, crystals that grow in
  still light and break at their own frequency, and living forms that feed, flash, swim, bond,
  divide with variation, go dormant, die and adapt over generations.
- **Twelve powers:** Hand, Seed, Strand, Pull, Twist, Pulse, Tune, Fracture, Fuse, Veil, Lens,
  Anchor, plus the Time Hand (pause, step, speed, rewind, checkpoints, branches). Every tool has
  a keyboard route and a no-drag mode. Undo restores the exact earlier state.
- **Strand Sight** and the **Lens**, for seeing relationships and invisible fields.
- **Five places and Genesis:** the Void Cradle, the Resonance Cathedral, the Fracture Garden,
  the Stillness Well, the Threshold, and the First Dark, where the laws wake as you find them.
- **Twelve Paradox Chambers**, each a question with more than one real answer.
- **The Discovery Atlas** (28 kinds of finding, plus new forms), the **Living Gallery** and
  **Compare**.
- **Saving that does not lose work:** autosave, save on close, two backups per save, daily copy
  of the whole save file, recovery from damage, export and import of universe files.
- **Pictures** in four sizes with optional title, explanation, measurements or caption.
- **Optional Claude** (your own key, off by default) for interpretation, suggestions, names,
  captions and reflections, behind a $10-a-month default limit that the game cannot exceed.
- **Synthesized sound** that follows the simulation: every concentration has a voice at its
  own rhythm.
- **Graphics levels** Low to Ultra; accessibility options for motion, flashing, contrast, text
  size and sound captions.
