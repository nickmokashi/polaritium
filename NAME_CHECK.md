# Name check: "Polaritium"

Preliminary search, 17 October 2026. **This is not legal clearance and not legal advice.** It
records what a basic search found so the name can be judged with the evidence in front of you.

## What was checked

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Exact name as a game, app or product | Nothing found under "Polaritium". |
| Domains (RDAP registry lookups) | polaritium.com, .net, .org and .game: not registered at the time of the check. polaritium.io: could not be confirmed either way. |
| Similar names in games | **Polarium**, a Nintendo puzzle game (Nintendo DS 2004/2005, Game Boy Advance sequel *Polarium Advance*); Nintendo filed a European trademark for the name in 2014. One letter pair apart, same industry. |
| Similar names in trademarks | **POLARIUM**, a registered US mark of a battery company (different goods). |

## Reading it

- No exact conflict turned up.
- The real risk is **Polarium**: a video game, owned by a large company, spelled almost the
  same. A trademark lawyer would weigh how alike they look and sound and that both are games.
- Nothing has been renamed. The name stays provisional, as the brief says, until you decide.

## If you want more certainty

A USPTO and EUIPO search for POLARITIUM and POLARIUM in classes 9 (software) and 41
(entertainment), and a short consultation with a trademark attorney before any public release.
