Polaritium is a game. Its universe, its rules and everything that happens in it are fictional.

Optional AI: Polaritium can use the Anthropic Claude API with a key you provide. Nothing is sent unless you switch AI on and ask for something. You are responsible for any charges on your Anthropic account; Polaritium's budget limits are a safeguard, not a guarantee of your Anthropic bill.

Your saves, settings and spending record stay on this computer, in your application data folder. Uninstalling Polaritium leaves them there, so a reinstall picks up where you left off. Delete the folder %APPDATA%\Polaritium to remove them.
